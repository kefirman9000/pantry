    Put an exact phrase in quotes: "large eggs".

    Put Pantry on your phone

    Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app, full screen, and works in the store even with no signal.

    1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (the square with an arrow).
    2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
    3. Tap Add. Pantry appears with your other apps.

    On iPhone the home-screen app keeps its own list, separate from Safari. Add your items once inside the app.

    Shopping mode

    How it works

    • Where do you shop? Type your ZIP code or town at the top. Nearby stores appear on the left.
    • What do you need? Add items on the left. Plain words work best, like eggs or chicken breast.
    • Prices check by themselves once you have a ZIP code and a list, and again whenever you add something. The big “Check prices” button under your list does it any time.
    • Pick which one. If an item could mean different products (like gravy), the page shows every store's options and asks which ones you want before planning. It remembers for next week.
    • Simple view (top of the page) hides the extras. Turn it off to see every store's price for each item and more tools.
    • At the store, press “Start shopping” for a big checklist you can tick off.
    • Double-check a price with the “Check at store” link or by tapping the picture, which is the store's own ad.

    Only sale prices from the weekly ads are shown, not every shelf price.
